Privacy Policy

HabitFlow is built so that the things you track never leave your phone. This page explains exactly what that means, and the one place where data does go elsewhere.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

The short version

  • Your habits never leave your device. There is no HabitFlow account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that your data is sent to.
  • We cannot see your data. Not your habits, not your streaks, not your notes — none of it reaches us.
  • The app shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads are the one part of the app that involves a third party.
  • You are asked before any tracking happens, and declining costs you nothing — every feature still works.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data. There is no copy anywhere else.

Who this policy is from

HabitFlow is developed by Sunny Yashvantkumar Patel ("we", "us"). This policy covers the HabitFlow iOS app and this website.

What HabitFlow stores, and where

Everything you create in HabitFlow is written to storage on your own device and to a shared container that lets the home screen widget read it. That includes:

  • Habits and tasks you create — names, descriptions, categories, schedules and reminder times
  • Your completion history, streaks, and freeze or vacation periods
  • Your XP, level, achievements and character progression
  • App settings such as your chosen language, card style, and sound and haptic preferences

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. HabitFlow has no backend server, no user accounts, and no cloud sync. If you export a backup, that file is written wherever you choose to save it and is under your control from that point on.

Advertising

HabitFlow shows optional rewarded ads — short videos you can choose to watch to unlock certain extras. You are never forced to watch one, and if an ad fails to load the app grants you the feature anyway rather than blocking you.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may process:

Your choices about tracking and consent

App Tracking Transparency. Before any tracking identifier is used, iOS asks for your permission, and HabitFlow only asks at the moment you choose to watch an ad — never on launch. If you decline, ads become non-personalised. Nothing is withheld from you for saying no. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in a region covered by the GDPR or equivalent rules, HabitFlow presents Google's consent form before requesting an ad, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Your choice is recorded and respected on every later ad request. You can reopen that form and change your decision at any time from Profile → Ad Privacy Choices, which appears in the app whenever those rules apply to you.

Notifications

Habit reminders and motivational notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. They are not push notifications, nothing is sent from a server, and the content of a reminder never leaves your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Analytics

HabitFlow contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not measure how you use the app, and we receive no usage reports. If Apple sends us aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics, that comes from Apple and not from anything inside the app.

Children

HabitFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app has no accounts and collects no personal details, we hold no information that could identify a child.

Your rights over your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you already have full control of it:

  • Access and export — use the backup export in the app to take a copy at any time.
  • Correct — edit or delete any habit, task or completion directly in the app.
  • Erase — deleting HabitFlow removes its data from your device. We hold no copy to delete on our side.

For advertising data held by Google, exercise your rights through Google using the link in the Advertising section above, or by changing your consent from Profile → Ad Privacy Choices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes that affect how data is handled will be reflected in the app before they take effect.
